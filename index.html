Kynurenine Pathway Dynamics and Affective Response to High- and Low-Intensity Resistance Exercise

A Randomized Crossover Trial

Finn Dreisigacker, Matthias Rißmayer, Fabian Fabritius, Niklas Joisten, Wilhelm Bloch, Florian Javelle

Download poster (PDF) Open in browser

A0 portrait · PDF

At a glance

Design
Randomized crossover trial
Participants
n = 24
Conditions
High-intensity interval resistance exercise (HIIRE)
Low-intensity resistance exercise (LIRE)
Blood sampling
Pre, post and 45 min post exercise
Outcomes
Kynurenine pathway ratios (KA/KYN, QA/KYN, QA/KA) and affective response (Feeling Scale)

Intensity matters: High-intensity interval resistance exercise induces greater immune activation than low-intensity resistance exercise 45 min post-workout.
